Identified Issues
What's Wrong With the Current Site
Critical
H1 Reads "Prints Supply"
The homepage H1 and several page titles still say "Prints Supply" — the old brand name. The rebrand to "Alabama Vinyl and Print Company" is not reflected in SEO-critical fields. Direct negative impact on local rankings.
Critical
Broken Pages — Prior Contractor Damage
A previous overseas contractor copy-pasted content from a reference site, including non-English text and stock videos. Multiple pages still have mismatched, placeholder, or foreign-language content.
High
10-Item Navigation — No Hierarchy
Main nav has 10+ links with no visual grouping or priority. Customers can't find the DTF ordering flow quickly. No clear primary CTA above the fold.
High
No DTF Customer Journey
Three distinct ordering flows exist (by size, gang sheet builder, pre-built upload) but there's no guidance on which to use. Customers land on product pages with no context, leading to confusion and abandoned carts.
High
Generic Impulse Theme — No Brand Identity
The site uses the Shopify Impulse theme with minimal customization. No brand story, no visual hierarchy, no emotional connection. Looks identical to hundreds of other Shopify stores.
Medium
404 Page Shows Placeholder Product
The 404 error page displays "EXAMPLE PRODUCT $29" — a default Shopify placeholder that was never replaced. Unprofessional and confusing for customers who land on broken links.
Medium
No LocalBusiness Schema Markup
No structured data for address, hours, or phone number. GBP listing exists but the site doesn't reinforce it with schema. Missing a direct path to #1 local ranking.
Low
Inconsistent Product Photography
Product images are flat, inconsistent in style and background. Not in scope to reshoot — flagged to Tiffany. High-quality mockups will be used in the rebuild where needed.
