Overview
What This Engagement Covers

A Custom Coach (acustomcoach.com) is a Denver/Boulder luxury ground transportation company established in 1988. The owner, John Hafer, engaged Speed of Now Productions to rebuild the company's website — a static 2013 Dreamweaver-era site currently scoring 2.5/10 against competitors — into a modern, premium digital presence.

Phase 1 focused entirely on research, competitive positioning, and infrastructure setup before any design or development work begins.

Work Completed

Client & Business Research
Complete
