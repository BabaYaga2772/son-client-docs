How It Works

  • 1
    Upload Your Design
    Choose your transfer type and upload your artwork. We accept PNG, PDF, PSD, AI, and EPS. Not sure about your file? Our AI upscaler handles most issues automatically.
  • 2
    We Print
    Your order goes to our professional DTF printers. Full-color, vibrant output on premium transfer film. Same day printing on orders placed before 2PM.
  • 3
    You Press
    Receive your transfers and heat press onto any fabric. Works on cotton, polyester, blends, and more. Easy peel, durable results, wash-safe.

Specs

File Requirements

  • ✓
    PNG Format Preferred We also accept PDF, PSD, AI, and EPS. PNG with transparent background is ideal.
  • ✓
    300 DPI Minimum Resolution Higher resolution = sharper print. 300 DPI at print size is the minimum. 600 DPI preferred for fine detail.
  • ✓
    Transparent Background Remove white backgrounds before uploading. Transparent PNG ensures clean edges on your garment.
  • ✓
    CMYK Color Mode Recommended CMYK gives the most accurate color matching. RGB files are accepted but colors may shift slightly in print.
  • ✓
    Outline All Fonts If your design includes text, convert fonts to outlines/paths to prevent font substitution issues.
